Alternatives, never additive: μx and Vx describe one horizontal condition; α, Vz and μz describe one axial condition. At Vx = 0 use Vz or μz for axial flow.

4. BET coefficients

Thrust, in-plane force, torque and rigid-hub moments are integrated over the active span and azimuth using local inflow and blade pitch. Profile drag uses 2D Gauss-Legendre quadrature (radial and azimuthal).

CT=TρAΩR2,CQ=QρAΩ2R3CQ=CQi+CQ0,CQi=kindλiCT+μzCT−μxCHi

The induced power factor kind sets the energy-balance induced torque; in-plane force splits into CHi and CH0.

7. Trim modes

9. Model scope

There is no blade flapping: hub moments Mx and My are rigid-hub values, not flapped-rotor moments. Figure of Merit is meaningful only in hover (μ = 0, μz = 0). Results are steady, quasi-static estimates.
