RotorCalculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

RotorCalculator is an Android engineering calculator provided by Flight Dyn.

Data collection and sharing

RotorCalculator does not collect or transmit personal data to Flight Dyn. It has no account login, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or cloud synchronization. Calculations run locally on your device.

Data stored on your device

Rotor geometries, engineering inputs, the active rotor, and display preferences are saved in the app's private local storage. You can delete saved custom rotors in Geometry. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to your device's operating system and backup settings.

Import and export

Import and export use Android's system file picker. You choose the files to import and the destination for exported rotor geometries, CSV data, and PNG plots. These files may contain the engineering inputs and rotor names you entered. Files saved outside the app remain under your control and are not removed when you uninstall RotorCalculator. If you choose a cloud-backed file provider, that provider handles the selected file under its own privacy policy.

Children

RotorCalculator is designed for engineering analysis. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. For questions about RotorCalculator privacy, contact flightdyn@gmail.com.
